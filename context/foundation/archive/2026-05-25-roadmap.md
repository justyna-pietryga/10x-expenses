## Vision recap

Ship the smallest useful monthly budgeting flow that proves people will import real bank statements, correct categories, and trust the resulting summary enough to use it again. The sequencing bias is `market-feedback`, so the roadmap favors one complete supported-bank workflow before widening bank coverage or polish.

The main planning constraint is `time`: the PRD allows a 3-week after-hours MVP, while the remaining work still spans setup, import, review, rules, and summary.

## North star

**S-03: User can save categorization rules from review and see a monthly summary against income and limits** — the north star here means the smallest end-to-end flow that proves the product works for a real user, so it is placed as early as its prerequisites allow.
